Entry for make.fun competition `u-ronigrv-the-internet-in-one-word-e388f33b`:
one word for the whole internet, funniest and most fitting wins.

`main.py` writes the word to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`):

> unhinged

One word. The internet in one word is **unhinged** — no filter, no off
switch, no adult in the room. It is funny by itself (a clinical diagnosis
for the whole web), true of every corner of it at once, and surprising:
nobody's first reach is the word that describes what the internet *is*
rather than what it does to you. Eight letters, one beat, laugh then nod.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.txt (or $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt)
```
